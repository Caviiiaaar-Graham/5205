# USD.AI protocol mechanics and design (USDai / sUSDai / CALIBER / loan book / redemptions / contracts / CHIP)

Research date: 2026-11-01. Scope: how the protocol documents its own mechanisms, with exact parameters and URLs, plus independent descriptions and critiques. Throughout, "[Official]" marks USD.AI's own docs/blog/site; "[Third-party]" marks independent sources.

Important orientation facts for the report writer:
- The protocol has had two materially different designs. **v1 (Mar 2025 – ~Jan 2026):** USDai backed by M0's $M/wM (tokenized T-bill dollar), minted from USDC/USDT; GPU collateral tokenized under the **CALIBER** framework (UCC Article 7 warehouse receipts / "GWRTs" as ERC-721 documents of title); loans underwritten by **FiLo Curators** posting first-loss capital; redemptions via a planned **QEV** auction. **v2 (2026):** USDai backed 1:1 by **PYUSD**; collateral rights via a **bankruptcy-remote Delaware SPV + first-priority lien** (UCC-1 filings) with "Loan NFTs" as on-chain records only; FiLo replaced by a **Barkr residual-value warranty**; redemptions via **30-day epoch FIFO queue** (QEV still "planned, not implemented"); governance token **CHIP** (TGE April 2026). Official blog posts from 2025 now carry an October-2026 banner: "CALIBER is no longer part of USD.AI's loan structure." — [Official: CALIBER Deep Dive, updated](https://usd.ai/blog/caliber-deep-dive)
- Trap to avoid: `usdai.gitbook.io` is an unrelated project ("USDAI" by GPU.NET, "1 USDAI = $1 of compute"), not Permian Labs' USD.AI. — [usdai.gitbook.io](https://usdai.gitbook.io/usdai)
- The Messari "A Valuation of USD.AI ($CHIP)" report (2026-03-02) now redirects to Blockworks and was **commissioned by USD.AI**. — [Blockworks/Messari report](https://app.blockworks.com/report/a-valuation-of-usdai-chip)

## Key question 1 — Minting USDai: accepted assets, chains, fees, minimums, KYC/geo, rebasing, NAV, peg

### Takeaway
USDai is a non-yielding, non-rebasing ERC-20 minted 1:1 against **PYUSD** (currently the only accepted mint asset) on Arbitrum (hub), redeemable for PYUSD; direct mint/redeem is "permissionless" with AML controls, after a short-lived (Apr 6 – Oct 1, 2026) whitelisting regime. The peg is maintained by full PYUSD backing plus DEX liquidity rather than by any algorithmic mechanism, and USDai carries no GPU-loan exposure.

Chains listed on that page: "Arbitrum, Ethereum, Base, or Plasma." — [docs.usd.ai/depositor/usdai](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/usdai.md)

### Inferences
- "NAV" is not a concept for USDai itself (fixed 1:1 to PYUSD); NAV/price-per-share applies to sUSDai (see Q2). USDai's peg relies on redeemability for PYUSD plus the PYUSD peg; the Oct 2025 premium episode shows the peg can break *upward* when minting is capped.
- The M0→PYUSD collateral switch most likely took place between late Dec 2025 and Mar 2026 (announced 2025-12-18; "transition took over a month" per 2026-03-26 post).
- The 10 bps redemption fee was tied to the Apr–Sep 2026 whitelisting regime; whether any protocol-level redemption fee survives the Oct 1, 2026 return to permissionless mint/redeem is not stated.

### Gaps
- No official minimum mint/redeem/stake size found (ICO minimum of $100 is for CHIP, not USDai).
- No explicit geographic block list for depositors (only sanctions language); the earlier M0 era accepted USDC/USDT per third parties but official pages from that period were not retrievable (web.archive.org is blocked in this environment).
- The current mint/redeem fee after 2026-10-01 is not documented.

## Key question 2 — sUSDai: what it is, leverage/looping, yield accrual, lockup/cooldown, APY history, relation to USDai

### Takeaway
sUSDai is a plain ERC-4626 vault share (ERC-7540 async redeem) over USDai — not itself leveraged — whose exchange rate rises as GPU-loan interest, PYUSD/T-bill reserve yield and the 4.5% PYUSD incentive accrue; it is not redeemable at par on demand but through a global 30-day epoch FIFO queue. Net APY has run ~6.9–7.8% through 2026 against an early marketing range of 13–17% and a 7.00% "CHIP Hurdle Rate" target.

### Cited Findings
- [Official] "sUSDai is the staked version of USDai, accruing yield from GPU-backed loans." "sUSDai is not a stablecoin. It is not instantly redeemable at par value. It represents a claim on underlying assets that include illiquid GPU-collateralized loans, and redemptions are subject to epoch-based timing constraints." Yield sources: "Interest payments are received from active AI infrastructure loans" and "Idle capital generates returns from Treasury Bill allocations." Conversion: sUSDai received = USDai × (Total sUSDai Supply / Total USDai in Vault); example: rate launched 1:1; vault with 110 USDai backing 100 sUSDai → rate 1.10; staking 100 USDai mints ~90.9 sUSDai. — [docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai.md)
- [Official] Technical definition: "Staked USDai (sUSDai) is a yield bearing ERC4626 (ERC7540 redeem) vault token that earns yield from USDai PYUSD emissions and LoanRouter loans… Unlike USDai, sUSDai is not a stablecoin, but is a free floating token, representing shares in an assortment of targeted lending positions." Staking = synchronous `deposit(assets, receiver)`/`mint(shares, receiver)`; unstaking = asynchronous `requestRedeem(shares, controller, owner)` then `redeem`/`withdraw`; "Redemptions are processed at the end of a fixed time window (e.g. 30 days)"; queue serviced by `serviceRedemptions(uint256 shares)`. — [Technical Protocol Overview](https://docs.usd.ai/technical-overview/technical-protocol-overview.md)
- [Official] NAV / two share prices: "The net asset value of sUSDai is the combined value of unallocated USDai and its loan positions. Loan positions are valued conservatively with the remaining balance of the loan, or optimistically with the remaining balance of the loan plus the interest accrued since last repayment. The deposit share price is computed from the optimistic net asset value, while the redemption share price is computed from the conservative net asset value. In general, the deposit share price is greater than or equal to the redemption share price. If there are no active loans, they are equal." Optimistic NAV: NAV_t = NAV_0 + ∫ Σ r_i·P_{i,u}·1{loan i performing} du + ∫ r_b·S_u du (r_b = USDai yield rate, S = USDai supply). Default settlement: V_j = frozen principal + accrued interest; I_j = max(F_j − R_j, 0) (residual value insurance, F_j = warrantied price "based on predefined RVI schedule", R_j = sale proceeds); Π_j = min(max(R_j, F_j), V_j); NAV_T = NAV_{T−} + Π_j − V_j. "No service provider has discretion to increase NAV or allocate additional yield outside the disclosed methodology." — [Technical Protocol Overview](https://docs.usd.ai/technical-overview/technical-protocol-overview.md)
- [Official] Rationale for two prices: "the deposit share price continuously prorates expected upcoming repayments across all loans in the book… The redemption share price, by contrast, is based on settled repayments and realized vault accounting"; "Some systems use a fixed redemption fee to address this. sUSDai instead uses dynamic forward pricing". — [sUSDai Withdrawal Estimates](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai/susdai-withdrawal-estimates.md)
- [Official] Net APY formula on the product page: "(PYUSD yield + (GPU loan yield − residual-value warranty cost)) × (1 − Protocol NIM)". Yield sources: loan interest (fixed or floating), income on escrowed capital, income on undeployed reserves. "The CHIP Hurdle Rate targets 7.00% net APY for sUSDai, topped up from the Yield Reserve when capital is available" (not guaranteed); the Yield Reserve "is funded over time from GLX gain-on-sale proceeds." — [usd.ai/susdai](https://usd.ai/susdai)
- [Official] "Are sUSDai returns guaranteed? No… Actual performance may be lower, zero, or negative." Exchange rate "reflects vault assets, net of fees, losses, and recoveries." — [FAQ 101](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/faq/usdai-and-susdai-101.md)
- [Official] Lockup/cooldown: no per-user cooldown; "Redemptions operate on a global 30-day epoch cycle. This is a protocol-wide timer, not a per-user countdown." Day 1–29 queue; Day 29 cutoff; Day 30 processing. "sUSDai keeps earning yield until the scheduled unstaking date. Allo Points stop accruing once an unstaking request is submitted." — [docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai.md); [Allo FAQ](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/faq/allo-points-and-alignment.md)
- [Official] Leverage is external, not internal: the depositor overview lists DeFi venues (Morpho, Fluid, Euler, Pendle, Curve, Uniswap V4) and "loops.gpu.credit (for current APYs)"; Season-3 Allo multipliers reward "sUSDai smart collateral (Fluid T2/T4)" and Pendle YT. — [Depositor overview](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/overview.md); [Allo Game: Flatiron Ends, Metropolitan Begins](https://usd.ai/blog/allo-game-flatiron-ends-metropolitan-begins). CoinDesk: yield "supported by GPU operator repayments rather than emissions or leverage loops." — [CoinDesk 2025-10-24](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2025/10/24/usdai-bridges-defi-and-ai-by-turning-stablecoins-into-loans-for-nvidia-gpus)

## Key question 3 — Reserve composition: "conservative assets", loan vs. liquid share, targets vs. actuals

### Takeaway
Reserves are PYUSD (previously M0 wM T-bill dollars) held idle plus GPU loans; officially the only hard constraints are a 90% maximum sUSDai utilization for new loans and that T-bill positions act as the liquidity buffer. Actual utilization rose from <1% (Oct 2025) to ~6% (Mar 2026) to roughly half of assets by mid/late 2026 (DefiLlama 2026-11-01: $274.3M tokens held vs $306.2M outstanding loan principal).

## Key question 5 — CALIBER and collateral legal mechanics: tokenization, liens, depreciation, insurance, default path, QEV and other proprietary terms

### Takeaway
CALIBER = "Collateralized Asset Ledger: Insurance, Bailment, Evaluation, and Redemption" — a v1 framework that minted ERC-721 "documents of title" (GPU Warehouse Receipts) under UCC Article 7 with a 14-day post-auction redemption window; since Oct 2026 USD.AI states CALIBER "is no longer part of USD.AI's loan structure", replaced by bankruptcy-remote SPVs with first-priority liens perfected by UCC-1 filings, Loan NFTs as records, Alliant property insurance, Barkr residual-value warranty (reinsured via Munich Re's aiSure per third parties), Aravolta monitoring and ITAD resale on default.

## Key question 6 — Redemption queue: FIFO vs QEV, fees, wait times, rationale vs instant redemption

### Takeaway
Today: a global 30-day epoch (requests Day 1–29, cutoff Day 29, processing Day 30) serviced strictly FIFO from all free USDai with no throttling, carrying unfilled balances to later epochs; loans are never liquidated to meet withdrawals and GLX loan sales are a new secondary cash source. QEV ("Queue Extractable Value") — sealed, zk-private proportional bidding for epoch liquidity with bids redistributed to patient stakers — is officially "planned but not yet implemented."

### Cited Findings
- [Official] Epoch mechanics: "Redemptions operate on a global 30-day epoch cycle. This is a protocol-wide timer, not a per-user countdown. Current withdrawal mechanics use a FIFO queue, with plans to transition to QEV in the future." Day 1–29: queue requests; Day 29: "Queue closes; no new requests accepted for current epoch"; Day 30: "Protocol processes queued redemptions from available cash". Processing: available USDai = vault USDai "not actively deployed in loans or reserved for imminent loan funding"; FIFO; "The protocol releases all available free USDai to service the queue. There is no throttling or partial release mechanism"; if insufficient, partial fills carry forward and "Unfulfilled requests carry to the next 30-day epoch"; "Active loans are not terminated early"; new loans only up to 90% utilization. QEV: "planned but not yet implemented… Implementation timeline and specifications are not finalized." — [docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai.md)
- [Official] FAQ: unstake via app.usd.ai/unstake; "Redemptions are automatically processed on fixed dates that occur every 30 days"; claim via "Pending Transactions → Withdraw"; "QEV is not active and… redemptions currently use a 30-day FIFO queue"; "governance and service providers cannot choose which users get redeemed"; a "limited instant liquidity buffer" exists. — [FAQ 101](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/faq/usdai-and-susdai-101.md)
- [Official] Product page: "Withdrawals run on a 30-day cycle, first in, first out… Payment comes from available cash, including loan repayments and undeployed reserves… unfilled requests carry into the next cycle." — [usd.ai/susdai](https://usd.ai/susdai)
- [Official] GLX as liquidity source: selling loans "can turn positions into cash to support sUSDai withdrawals, instead of relying only on scheduled repayments", "not a guarantee of an immediate sale or immediate withdrawal"; target "weighted-average holding period of approximately 90 days"; "Gains on sale may also be used to support yield." — [GLX docs](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai/glx.md)
- [Official] QEV design page: "a novel DeFi Primitive for Long-Dated Redemptions and Low-Liquidity Collateral"; liquidity released in 30-day epochs (Δ); bids private via zero-knowledge proofs; "Allocated Liquidity = (Absolute Bid / Σ Absolute Bids) × Δ"; FIFO if no bids; unredeemed proceeds roll into new loans or T-bills; bids ("queue bribes") earned by protocol and redistributed to passive stakers (split unspecified); UI tiers: Low 0–10 bps → 2–5 epochs, ~25% chance; Mid 10–50 bps → 1–2 epochs, ~60%; High 50–100+ bps → same epoch, ~95% ("Instant Redemption"); worked example Δ=$100,000: Alice $750k deposit bids $75 → $5,660.38 (5.66%); Bob $50k bids $125 → $9,433.96 (9.43%); Charlie $150k bids $1,125 → $84,905.66 (84.91%); Δ=$250,000: Alice bids $6,000 → $234,375 (93.75%).
