## KQ1. Credit / counterparty risk of neocloud borrowers

### Takeaway
USD.AI's loan book is concentrated in small, loss-making or OTC-listed neoclouds (e.g., QumulusAI carries going-concern language; Sharon AI is OTC-quoted) and the protocol explicitly underwrites the collateral, not the borrower; no default has been reported through Oct 2026, but the book is only ~15 months old, and October 2026 CoreWeave CDS stress (>1,000 bp) plus Musk's "200+ neoclouds will fail" warning show the sector's credit cycle has not yet been tested.

### Cited Findings
- Underwriting is asset-based: "primary credit anchor is the recoverability of the collateral"; max 80% LTV at origination vs verified acquisition cost; 3-year straight-line amortization (up to 5 years to match offtake); fixed, prepayable rates set by offtake quality: Tier 1 (investment-grade offtaker) 7–9%, Tier 2 (non-IG multi-year contract) 10–12%, Tier 3 (on-demand/spot) 12–15%; 3-month debt-service reserve funded from borrower equity; quarterly 1.15x DSCR test, two consecutive breaches -> 30-day cure -> default and acceleration (post dated 15 May 2026, updated Oct 2026). — [USD.AI Underwriting & Risk Management](https://usd.ai/blog/usdai-underwriting-and-risk-management)
- CEO David Choi (interview published 7 Nov 2025): USD.AI "does not assess who borrowers are or what they do, only the GPU collateral"; borrowers must pay every 30 days or enter default; "liquidation is not used; only default determinations." — [ChainCatcher interview via Bitget (Chinese)](https://www.bitget.com/zh-CN/news/detail/12560605052744)
- Loans are non-recourse to the operator's balance sheet; recourse is limited to the collateral/SPV, with a parent "Limited Guaranty" covering bad acts (fraud etc.). — [USD.AI docs (llms-full)](https://docs.usd.ai/llms-full.txt)
- Galaxy Research (13 Jul 2026, Lucas Tcheyan): ~95% of the loan book is backed by long-term offtake contracts rather than spot rental; repayment "depends more on the offtake counterparty than on daily rental rates"; neoclouds have "contracted cash flows but thinner balance sheets." — [Galaxy "Inference Capital Markets"](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/inference-capital-markets-ai-compute-gpu-futures-onchain-crypto)
- Borrower example, QumulusAI (SEC 10-Q/A for period ended 30 Jun 2026): USD.AI protocol loans of $18.9M on balance sheet ($6.9M current, $12.0M non-current; none at 31 Dec 2025); six-month net loss $(72.4)M on revenue $10.1M; unrestricted cash $20.0M; Note 1 states factors "raise substantial doubt about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern," with management's mitigation plan including "completing an additional $15,300,000 draw under the USD.AI protocol." — [QumulusAI 10-Q/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002084026/000162828026059295/qmls-20260630.htm)
- QumulusAI's facility was announced as a $500M non-recourse facility on 9 Oct 2025. — [QumulusAI press release](https://www.qumulusai.com/articles/qumulusai-secures-500m-non-recourse-financing-facility-through-usdai-to-accelerate-ai-infrastructure-growth)
- Borrower example, Sharon AI (announced 22 Jan 2026): up to US$500M asset-backed non-recourse facility; expected to begin drawing in 1Q CY2026 for $65M of initial GPU deployments; followed a $100M convertible note raise (Dec 2025); January coverage described Sharon AI as OTC-quoted (SHAZ, SHAZW), while a March 2026 conference summary referenced a $125M Nasdaq raise (unverified). — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260122439371/en/Sharon-AI-Secures-up-to-US%24500M-Debt-Facility-from-USD.AI-to-Support-GPU-Backed-AI-Infrastructure-Expansion-in-Australia-and-Asia-Pacific); [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/sharon-ai-secures-500m-debt-facility-from-usdai/); [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/instant-alerts/sharonai-holdings-pitches-australia-neocloud-ai-compute-details-770m-funding-at-redchip-conference-2026-03-22/)
- Other named facilities/borrowers: Quantum Solutions $200M; BSQ Capital Partners $300M APAC JV (6 Jul 2026); Duos Edge AI $98.1M (5 Jun 2026); NexGen Cloud $34M (25 Jun 2026); Bullish $100M stablecoin credit commitment (28 Aug 2026); a record $128.9M facility for 32 NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 systems in British Columbia; Crucible Capital $26.8M draw. — [USD.AI docs media list](https://docs.usd.ai/llms-full.txt); [Quantum Solutions](https://usd.ai/blog/quantum-solutions-usdai-financing); [Unite.ai $128.9M](https://www.unite.ai/usd-ai-secures-record-128-9m-asset-backed-financing-for-gpu-deployment/); [Unite.ai Bullish](https://www.unite.ai/usd-ai-secures-100m-stablecoin-debt-facility-from-bullish-to-finance-gpu-backed-loans/); [Crucible](https://old.usd.ai/stories/crucible-capital-26m-gpu-backed-loan)

- Track record: "No defaults, liquidations, or missed payments have been reported" (LlamaRisk monthly update, 17 Jul 2026); Zhitong (20 Oct 2026) reports no defaults and one prepayment after ~15 months. — [Aave forum thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260); [Zhitong](http://news.10jqka.com.cn/20261020/c680901288.shtml)

- Critique (Pine Analytics, 28 Jan 2026): hyperscalers finance capex at sub-5%, so USD.AI's "realistic borrowers are the smaller long tail"; borrowers pay 15–20% APR and GPU collateral depreciates quickly, so "recovery in default may fall far below principal." — [Pine Analytics "The Bear Case for $CHIP"](https://pineanalytics.substack.com/p/the-bear-case-for-chip)
- Sector stress signal: CoreWeave 5-year CDS exceeded 1,000 bp on 15 Oct 2026 (peak ~1,194.5 bp), narrowing to ~1,041 bp by 27 Oct 2026; coverage cites ~63% implied 5-year default probability and ~16% yields on 2031 notes. — [ROIC 15 Oct 2026](https://www.roic.ai/news/ai-debt-fears-surge-as-tech-default-insurance-hits-record-highs-10-15-2026); [ROIC 27 Oct 2026](https://www.roic.ai/news/coreweave-cds-narrows-to-lowest-in-over-a-week-but-credit-stress-persists-10-27-2026)
- Elon Musk (25 Oct 2026), announcing SpaceX's $40B GPU financing, said debt markets have "almost entirely shut off" for companies without substantial revenue and predicted a neocloud shakeout; coverage framed it as "200+ neoclouds" at risk. — [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/elon-musk-neoclouds-guppy-level-death-debt-markets); [Star-Advertiser/AP](https://www.staradvertiser.com/2026/10/25/breaking-news/musk-spacex-raised-40b-in-debt-deal-predicts-neocloud-shakeout/)
- Team's bull framing: banks "rarely finance the GPU cluster itself"; USD.AI's essay acknowledges its model moves asset-level risk "outside the regulated banking perimeter... fueling the growth of the shadow banking sector." — [USD.AI "Why Banks Can't Fund the GPU Revolution"](https://usd.ai/insights/banks-finance-gpu-credit)

### Inferences
- Because underwriting is collateral-first and non-recourse, borrower insolvency converts directly into a collateral-liquidation problem; the DSCR/DSRA covenants buy time but the ultimate loss-given-default is a function of GPU resale value, not borrower equity.
- A public borrower (QumulusAI) with going-concern doubt and a plan that depends on further USD.AI draws is a concrete, verifiable data point for the "adverse selection" critique (borrowers who cannot access bank/IG markets).
- With 13 borrowers and a largest loan of $98.1M against ~$205M deployed (June 2026), single-name concentration was ~48% at that date (derived).

### Gaps
- No public per-loan performance data (DSCR levels, reserve balances) — only aggregate "no defaults" statements.
- Facility terms (rate, LTV) for QumulusAI/Sharon AI are not disclosed in captured filings; the QumulusAI debt note was truncated.
- Sharon AI's Nasdaq status is unverified.

## KQ2. Collateral risk — GPU depreciation, resale, obsolescence, repossession, insurance

### Takeaway
Independent estimates of H100 residual value diverge widely (used cards $6K–$25K in 2026 vs ~$50K peak in mid-2024), useful-life views range from Burry's 2–3 years to CoreWeave's 6; USD.AI mitigates with 20%+ equity, 3-year amortization, a Barkr residual-value warranty (reinsured, with carve-outs including suspension in a "market-wide shock"), and SPV-level enforcement — but Galaxy notes enforcement "has not been tested through a full distressed recovery" and no B200/B300 secondary-market clearing data exists.

### Cited Findings
- Resale market, 2026: Mercatus puts 18–30-month-old used H100 SXM5 at $22.5K–$25.5K, falling to $12K–$22.5K at 36 months; ClusterBid reports $15K–$19K in Q2 2026 (~42% residual at 24–30 months); Luminix cites $18K–$22K in early 2026; IntuitionLabs lists $6K–$22K used. New H100s remain ~$25K–$35K. — [Mercatus](https://www.mercatus-ai.com/blog/h100-resale-value); [ClusterBid](https://clusterbid.com/blog/secondary-gpu-market-2026-used-h100-prices-resale-value-trading-volume); [Luminix](https://www.useluminix.com/reports/industry-analysis/deep-dive-on-the-neocloud-gpu-rental-industry-coreweave-lambda-crusoe); [IntuitionLabs](https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/nvidia-ai-gpu-pricing-guide)
- Hashrate Index (8 Apr 2026): used H100 peaked near $50K in mid-2024 then "fell sharply"; used 8-GPU H100 server ~$150K–$180K; A100 80GB PCIe ~$18.9K, 40GB ~$7.8K; expects used A100 prices to fall another 10–15% in 2026; cloud H100 rental fell from $7–10/hr (2023) to $2–4/hr (late 2025); CoreWeave's 2022-era H100 contracts reportedly rebooked at 95% of original pricing. — [Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/used-gpu-market-pricing-deprecation-secondary-ai/)
- Useful-life debate: hyperscalers use ~5–6 years (Amazon cut a subset to 5 in 2025; Meta extended to 5.5); neoclouds: Lambda 5, Nebius 4, CoreWeave 6. Michael Burry (Nov 2025) argues a 2–3-year economic life and ~$176B of understated depreciation 2026–28: "Massively ramping capex through purchase of Nvidia chips/servers on a 2-3 yr product cycle should not result in the extension of useful lives." — [Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/used-gpu-market-pricing-deprecation-secondary-ai/); [Techstrong](https://techstrong.ai/features/tech-investor-michael-burry-alleges-accounting-irregularities-at-hyperscalers-in-pursuit-of-ai-boom/); [Motley Fool 11 Nov 2025](https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/11/11/michael-burrys-latest-warning-could-be-bad-news-fo)
- Galaxy (Jul 2026): USD.AI loans amortize over 3 years "against a claimed seven-year life"; a faster hardware cycle would narrow that gap; "older chips have continued to hold value"; Barkr valuations "are reinsured by Munich Re"; "neither the coverage nor offchain enforcement has been tested through a wave of defaults." — [Galaxy](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/inference-capital-markets-ai-compute-gpu-futures-onchain-crypto)
- Stablewatch (23 Oct 2025): "accelerated depreciation of its GPU collateral [is] the most significant asset-specific risk"; hardware refresh cycles 12–18 months; ITAD remarketing firms charge ~30% of gross sale; export controls could create a used-hardware glut; CUDA-moat erosion treated as low probability. — [Stablewatch deep dive](https://www.stablewatch.io/research/usd-ai-deep-dive)
- Third-party explainers assume ~20%/yr GPU depreciation and ~3-year useful life. — [bbx CHIP report (Chinese)](https://bbx.com/zh-Hant/article/531596); [BingX](https://bingx.com/en/learn/article/what-is-usdai-chip-infrafi-gpu-backed-yield-how-does-it-work)
- Eligible collateral (May/Oct 2026): RTX Pro 6000, B200/B300, GB200/GB300; H100/H200 "case-by-case"; must sit in Tier III/IV data centers; capital released only after independent on-site verification; 80% LTV expected to fall to ~65% by end of year one. — [USD.AI Underwriting](https://usd.ai/blog/usdai-underwriting-and-risk-management)
- Team statement (2026): collateral is a "managed shortlist" of NVIDIA chips with large secondary markets, plus chips with IG offtake backstops (e.g., AMD); eligibility changes will be disclosed. — [Aave forum thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- Insurance: P&C (flood/fire/theft) required at close; USD.AI docs name Alliant for insurance, Barkr for residual value, Aravolta for hardware monitoring. Barkr warranty (announced 6 Feb 2026; "Barker" rebranded "Barkr"): covers "part of a shortfall" when servers sell below an agreed floor; required where offtaker is non-IG or compute is sold on-demand; costs 150 bps/yr netted from yield; does NOT apply to a sale of the SPV as a whole; "may be suspended during a market-wide shock"; backed by an "A-rated institutional reinsurer." — [USD.AI docs](https://docs.usd.ai/usdai/how-it-works); [USD.AI Barkr story](https://usd.ai/stories/usdai-barker-insured-gpu-loan-coverage); [USD.AI Underwriting](https://usd.ai/blog/usdai-underwriting-and-risk-management)
- A competing RVI vendor says the aiSure program behind such warranties publishes limits "up to USD 50m per model, subject to co-insurance, deductibles and sub-limits" (marketing-influenced source). — [American Compute](https://www.amcompute.com/gpu-residual-value-insurance)
- Earlier structure (2025): Evertas insurance and Blockware appraisals; FiLo curators as first-loss; 7-day English auction on default; LlamaRisk: "GPUs are not appraised before being liquidated, and market prices are provided by USD.AI's hardware partners." — [Blockworks Research, 18 Jul 2025](https://app.blockworks.com/report/synthetic-dollars-collateralized-by-gpu-cash-flows?from=bwr); [Aave forum (Chaos/LlamaRisk Oct 2025)](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- Default process preference: keep the SPV operating or sell it whole; selling servers individually is a fallback; USD.AI "does not aim to own or operate GPU clusters." — [USD.AI docs](https://docs.usd.ai/llms-full.txt)
- Zhitong critique (20 Oct 2026): "no real secondary-market liquidation data for B300 or other next-generation GPUs"; models liquidation at 30% of spot and a "default–liquidation–price spiral"; spot H100 fell from $3.5/hr (early 2025) to $1.29 (early 2026); spot B200 fell from $6.1 to $4.37 (Jun–Jul 2026). — [Zhitong](http://news.10jqka.com.cn/20261020/c680901288.shtml)
- Lex Sokolin (26 Aug 2025): A100 superseded ~2 years after launch; AMD MI250 lasted 18 months; product is "priced like a GPU junk bond." — [Fintech Blueprint](https://lex.substack.com/p/ai-gpu-backed-stablecoins-blend-ai)

### Inferences
- The Barkr carve-outs (SPV sale excluded; suspension in market-wide shock) mean the warranty is weakest precisely in the correlated-default scenario that matters most for a pooled lender.
- Shifting eligible collateral toward Blackwell (B200/GB200) reduces H100 obsolescence exposure but increases reliance on an untested resale market.
- Even at the conservative Mercatus curve, a 36-month-old H100 retains ~35–65% of new price; combined with 3-year full amortization, a performing loan is likely over-collateralized late in life, so losses concentrate in early-life defaults or technology shocks.

### Gaps
- Floor values used by Barkr and the reinsurer identity are not disclosed in primary sources (Munich Re is cited by Galaxy and a Substack, not by USD.AI itself).
- No data on time-to-sell or realized recovery because no USD.AI loan has defaulted.

## KQ3. Liquidity risk — redemption queue, run dynamics, secondary discounts, depeg history

### Takeaway
No USDai/sUSDai depeg below $1 has been documented; the observed anomaly was a persistent PREMIUM ($1.03–$1.06, Aug–Oct 2025) caused by supply caps. The real liquidity event was the March 2026 queue: ~$127M of sUSDai redemptions queued on 6 Mar 2026 (vs ~$330M unallocated USDai) after the points program ended, i.e., ~38% of sUSDai TVL requested exit in one epoch; it was absorbed because the book was still mostly cash. The protocol will not liquidate loans to meet redemptions, so a similar run at 80–90% utilization would simply extend the queue.

### Cited Findings
- Mechanics: sUSDai redemptions on a global 30-day epoch, FIFO, cutoff day 29; "During periods of high utilization, redemption queues may extend across multiple epochs"; "The protocol does not prematurely liquidate loans to satisfy withdrawals"; max utilization for new loans 90%; QEV (auction priority) is "planned but not implemented... specifications are not finalized." — [USD.AI docs](https://docs.usd.ai/usdai/how-it-works); [USD.AI docs sUSDai](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai); [QEV page](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/susdai/queue-extractable-value)
- Official disclosure: "sUSDai is not a stablecoin. It is not instantly redeemable at par value"; "When cash is short, unfilled requests carry into later cycles and payment can take longer"; "Holders can lose principal." — [USD.AI docs](https://docs.usd.ai/llms-full.txt); [USD.AI May 2026 recap](https://usd.ai/blog/usdai-may-2026-recap)
- Queue history: serviced once on 19 Sep 2025 (83 requests in one batch; 142 submitted in total) as of Oct 2025; code reverts if `amountProcessed > _usdaiBalance()`. — [Chaos Labs/LlamaRisk, Aave forum, Oct 2025](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- March 2026 event: "approximately 130M in sUSDai redemptions against 342M TVL," attributed to the end of the points program (LlamaRisk, 3 Apr 2026); peak ~$127M queued on 6 Mar 2026 against ~$330M unallocated USDai; sUSDai peaked at 320M in Feb 2026; USDai supply fell from 524M peak (Jan 2026) to 299M on Arbitrum (31 Mar 2026). — [Aave forum thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- Premium episodes: USDai above $1.00 since mid-Aug 2025, peaking ~6% in early Oct 2025 ("between $1.03 and $1.06 across DEXs"), ~3% later; sUSDai premium peaked ~4.2%, moderating to ~2%; cause: 500M mint cap / $581.2M deposit cap "filled." Chaos Labs: a premium makes liquidations less attractive because liquidators must repurchase above par. — [Chaos Labs & LlamaRisk, Aave forum Oct 2025](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- "Since November [2025], USDai has traded closer to its Dollar peg" after the cap was removed; Chaos Labs projected (not observed) a 2–5 bp USDai discount after a redemption-fee change (Apr 2026). — [Aave forum thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- Secondary depth (Jul 2026): USDai and sUSDai can each be swapped for ~$13.8–13.9M USDC within 7.5% price impact; a $100M sUSDai liquidity facility with Fluid was announced. Oct 2025 depth: USDai Arbitrum ~$32M; sUSDai Arbitrum ~$40M with "only about 10% of the total DEX liquidity... composed of sUSDai itself." — [Aave forum thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260)
- KYC gating: from 6 Apr 2026 direct mint/redeem of USDai restricted to KYC'd market makers and institutions; retail route is DEX/CEX purchase then stake. — [USD.AI mint/redeem upgrade](https://usd.ai/insights/usdai-mint-redeem-upgrade); [USD.AI docs](https://docs.usd.ai/depositor/usdai)
- Pendle PT implied discount: PT-sUSDai traded ~0.913 with ~9.5% implied yield (Oct 2026), which "only absorbs about an 8.7% drop in sUSDai NAV." — [Zhitong](http://news.10jqka.com.cn/20261020/c680901288.shtml)
- CEO (Nov 2025): the 30-day wait is "sUSDai's main risk"; arbitrageurs buying below par and redeeming at face is the peg mechanism; contrasted with Ethena's isolated pools. — [ChainCatcher/Bitget](https://www.bitget.com/zh-CN/news/detail/12560605052744)
- Galaxy: "A liquid token backed by loans that amortize over three years is an asset-liability mismatch." Blockworks Research: system behaves "much like asset-backed commercial paper with a built-in, market-driven gate." — [Galaxy](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/inference-capital-markets-ai-compute-gpu-futures-onchain-crypto); [Blockworks Research](https://app.blockworks.com/report/synthetic-dollars-collateralized-by-gpu-cash-flows?from=bwr)
- Delphi (6 May 2025, a consulting engagement with MetaStreet): redemption is the central challenge for synthetic dollars with illiquid collateral; cites Usual's USD0++ depeg where LP incentives "were insufficient." — [Delphi report summary](https://members.delphidigital.io/reports/usd-ai-financing-the-future-of-ai-infra)
- Public-company risk factor (iPower S-1, 2026): sUSDai redemptions "may be asynchronous, queued, subject to timelocks and serviced periodically" and "the redemption share price may be lower than the deposit share price." — [iPower S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001830072/000168316826005462/ipower_s1.htm)
- Loan repayments return ~3–4% of principal monthly (3-year amortization) — the only organic liquidity at high utilization. — [Stablewatch](https://www.stablewatch.io/research/usd-ai-deep-dive)

### Inferences
- March 2026 was a benign stress test only because utilization was ~6% (loans $18.1M); the same 38%-of-TVL request at the stated 80% utilization target would require 8–12 months of amortization cash flow to clear, implying multi-epoch queues and a secondary-market discount rather than a formal depeg.
- Structural gating means "depeg" risk manifests as PT/secondary discounts and queue length, not as a USDai price break; USDai itself (PYUSD-backed) is low-risk, sUSDai is the risk-bearing instrument.

### Gaps
- No time series of sUSDai secondary price vs NAV was found; no formal depeg episode documented.
- How the March 2026 queue cleared epoch-by-epoch is not described in sources.

## KQ4. Leverage risk — sUSDai looping (Pendle PT/YT, Morpho, Aave)

### Takeaway
Leverage around sUSDai is real but, as of 2026, modest in size: Pendle SY contracts held ~54% of USDai and ~82% of Arbitrum sUSDai (Oct 2025), USD.AI itself promoted 30% PT yields with >$300M TVL (Sep 2025), and Morpho markets lend at 86–91.5% LLTV with loop APYs cited up to 27%; Aave's risk providers explicitly flagged that USDai borrow demand will be dominated by sUSDai loops and that "a synchronized unwind could pressure sUSDai."

### Cited Findings
- USD.AI (Sep 2025): "Pendle fixed rate PT yields for USDai and sUSDai are now at 30% with over $300m worth of TVL." — [USD.AI on X](https://x.com/USDai_Official/status/1971976435696050425)
- Pendle pools offered 12–37% APY with 12x–25x Allo Points multipliers until 20–21 Nov 2025; Allo multipliers for YT-USDai were cut from 30x to 15x on 18 Nov 2025. — [CoinMarketCap USDai updates](https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/usdai/latest-updates/); [Pendle on X](https://x.com/pendle_fi/status/1990597409190392087)

## KQ6. Legal and regulatory risk (GENIUS Act, securities law, US persons, MAS, UCC enforcement)

### Takeaway
USDai (PYUSD-wrapped, non-yielding) sits adjacent to the GENIUS regime via its reserve asset, while sUSDai — a yield-bearing vault share over a private loan book — falls outside the Act, outside the SEC's April 2025 "covered stablecoin" safe harbor, and squarely into Howey-style analysis; Singapore's September 2026 MAS proposal would likewise bar interest on MAS-regulated stablecoins, leaving yield products in the DPT regime. Collateral enforcement moved from an untested UCC Article 7 "CALIBER" NFT design to conventional perfected security interests over bankruptcy-remote SPVs, which Galaxy notes is still untested in a distressed recovery.

### Cited Findings
- GENIUS Act (P.L. 119-27, July 2025) Sec. 4: "no permitted payment stablecoin issuer... shall pay the holder of any payment stablecoin any form of interest or yield... solely in connection with the holding, use, or retention"; "holder" undefined, leaving the three-party/exchange model open. — [CRS In Focus IF13174](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/IF/HTML/IF13174.html)
- Synthetic dollars without full fiat backing are outside the Act's scope; Forbes (15 Jun 2026) argues the structure "exempts USDe from the Act's definition, creating a significant regulatory gap." — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/06/15/ethenas-usde-pays-yield-legally-and-the-genius-act-has-no-answer-for-it/); [HTX commentary](https://www.htx.com/en-us/news/428511/)
- A Notice of Proposed Rulemaking published 2 Mar 2026 introduces a rebuttable presumption that arrangements where an issuer pays an affiliate/third party who routes yield to holders violate the prohibition (anti-evasion). — [Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/can-you-still-earn-yield-on-stablecoins-after-the-occs-genius-act-rule/)
- Cap Labs' founder: stablecoins paying yield to retail holders are restricted under GENIUS, so yield is kept in a separate staked wrapper (same pattern as sUSDai). — [Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/cap-labs-eigenlayer)
- SEC Division of Corporation Finance (4 Apr 2025): "covered stablecoins" (1:1 USD-redeemable, low-risk liquid reserves) are not securities; guidance explicitly excludes yield-bearing, algorithmic and other stablecoins; commentators infer yield-bearing coins "could be securities." Figure's YLDS became the first SEC-registered yield-bearing stablecoin (18 Feb 2025). — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/349676/sec-declares-covered-stablecoins-like-usdt-and-usdc-are-not-securities-no-registration-needed-for-minting-or-redeeming); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2025/04/usd-linked-stablecoins-are-not-securities-latest-sec-staff-guidance); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/313431/stablecoins-not-securities-sec-yield-bearing-tokens-different)
- Howey mapping (opinion, Lex Sokolin, Aug 2025): investment of money, common enterprise (pooled GPUs), expectation of profit (15% APY), efforts of others — "look very much like traditional securities"; product "may be inappropriate for retail investors." — [Fintech Blueprint](https://lex.substack.com/p/ai-gpu-backed-stablecoins-blend-ai)
- Stablewatch: sUSDai "may be classified as a security in key jurisdictions"; the CALIBER design "relies on an untested application of UCC Article 7 that has yet to be challenged in court." — [Stablewatch](https://www.stablewatch.io/research/usd-ai-deep-dive)
- CALIBER retired: Galaxy (Jul 2026) — "The protocol retired its earlier CALIBER framework. Enforcement now runs through offchain loan documents... has not been tested through a full distressed recovery." — [Galaxy](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/inference-capital-markets-ai-compute-gpu-futures-onchain-crypto)
- Current lien structure: "first-priority perfected security interest" over all SPV assets plus 100% SPV equity pledge; colocation operator must sign a lien waiver; UCC/lien/judgment searches; bankruptcy-remote SPV with independent manager; lends only where "hardware title can be perfected and security interest enforcement is reliable"; OFAC screening of the borrower group. — [USD.AI Underwriting](https://usd.ai/blog/usdai-underwriting-and-risk-management)

- US persons: no explicit US-person exclusion found in captured docs; Terms of Service last modified 12 Jan 2026; from 6 Apr 2026 direct mint/redeem limited to KYC'd market makers/institutions; AML controls "on the app frontend and in the contract." A US-listed company (iPower) discloses holding USDai and sUSDai as treasury assets. — [Aave forum thread](https://governance.aave.com/t/arfc-onboard-usdai-susdai-to-aave-v3-arbitrum-instance/23260); [USD.AI docs](https://docs.usd.ai/usdai/how-it-works); [iPower S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001830072/000168316826005462/ipower_s1.htm)

USD.AI is long AI-capex continuation: its loans are fixed-rate 3-year amortizers to sub-IG neoclouds whose ability to pay depends on offtake contracts (~95% of book) and, at the margin, spot GPU rental rates that fell ~60% from early-2025 to early-2026 (spot H100) before 1-year contract rates rebounded ~40% (Oct 2025–Mar 2026); falling policy rates would lower reserve yield and TradFi competitors' cost of funds without lowering USD.AI's fixed loan income, while a capex slowdown hits recovery values and refinancing of borrowers simultaneously.

### Inferences
- Scenario — AI capex slows: offtake renewals fail → DSCR breaches → defaults into a falling resale market where the Barkr warranty may be suspended ("market-wide shock"); sUSDai NAV write-downs would propagate to Pendle PT discounts and Morpho/Aave loops; redemption queues extend because loans are not liquidated.
- Scenario — GPU rental prices fall but capex continues: Tier 3 (spot) borrowers (12–15% loans) are most exposed; ~95% contracted book cushions near-term cash flow but raises rollover risk at contract expiry (CoreWeave's DDTL 5.5 shows ~2-year gap between contract and loan maturity as an industry norm).
- Scenario — rates fall: USD.AI's fixed loan book keeps earning 10.46% weighted while undeployed-cash yield (~1/3 of net yield today) compresses; relative attractiveness vs sUSDS/T-bill coins rises, but TradFi lenders' cheaper funding erodes USD.AI's origination edge for better borrowers. Scenario — rates rise: fixed-rate book underperforms, PT discounts widen, and borrower refinancing stress increases.
