## Key Question 1 — Founders, core team, Permian Labs ↔ MetaStreet lineage, parent/foundation/DAO

### Takeaway
USD.AI is developed by **Permian Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation headquartered in New York**, co-founded in 2021 by **David Choi (CEO), Conor Moore (COO) and Ivan Sergeev**; the same three people founded **MetaStreet** (NFT/RWA lending, 2021), and Permian Labs is the company behind both — MetaStreet was its first protocol and USD.AI's primitives (ATM, LCT, Yield Pass) evolved out of it. Since January 2026 the protocol has an off-chain legal steward, the **USD.AI Foundation** (a Cayman Islands foundation company, now styled "CHIP Foundation" in the Terms of Service), with BVI/Delaware subsidiaries acting as loan agent/servicer, and a CHIP-token DAO for governance.

## Key Question 3 — Origin story, stated thesis, and dated launch timeline (announcement, beta, public launch)

### Takeaway
USD.AI was first revealed publicly on **18 March 2025** (X thread) and **25 March 2025** (first blog post, "USD.AI is Powered By M^0") as a T-bill-backed synthetic dollar whose staked version funds GPU/DePIN loans via MetaStreet; **private beta on Arbitrum went live 10 June 2025**, the **public launch on Arbitrum was 21 August 2025**, PYUSD replaced M0's $M as backing (migration finalised 26 Feb 2026), the **CHIP token launched 21 April 2026**, and governance went live 19 Oct 2026. The stated thesis is to build the "interest rate of intelligence" — an on-chain credit market in which GPUs are financed like commodities — framed by investors as "InfraFi".

## Key Question 4 — Legal structure: issuer jurisdiction, SPVs, UCC Article 7/9, Cayman/BVI/Delaware entities, licensing, US-person restrictions, named legal/structuring partners

### Takeaway
The protocol's ToS counterparty is a **Cayman Islands foundation company (USD.AI/CHIP Foundation)** governed by Cayman law with AAA arbitration; loans are made to **bankruptcy-remote Delaware LLC borrower SPVs** (Independent Manager: Gemsbok Partners LLC) with a **true-sale/first-priority-lien structure perfected by UCC filings**, administered by **GPU Finance Ltd. (BVI)** and **GPU Servicer LLC** and escrowed at **Wilmington Trust**. The original 2025 "CALIBER" design relied on **UCC Article 7 electronic warehouse receipts (GWRTs) tokenised as NFTs with a precautionary Article 9 security interest**; by October 2026 USD.AI states CALIBER "is no longer part of USD.AI's loan structure". No law firm or legal-opinion author is publicly named; no licence is claimed; U.S. persons are not barred from the protocol by the ToS but were excluded from the ICO unless accredited.

### Inferences
- The legal architecture migrated during 2025–2026 from a "tokenised title" model (Article 7 e-documents of title held by a Tokenizing Agent, bailment back to the borrower) to a conventional equipment-ABS/secured-loan model (borrower-owned Delaware SPV, true sale from parent, first-priority Article 9-perfected lien, escrow, lien waivers, step-in rights), with the Loan NFT reduced to an on-chain record. - The Cayman foundation + BVI agent + Delaware operator pattern matches the standard offshore DeFi "foundation/labs" split; the ToS's Cayman-law/AAA-New-York arbitration combination implies the structure anticipates U.S. users.

### Gaps
- No law firm, structuring counsel, or author of the UCC legal memoranda is publicly named in any source found.
- No regulatory licence, registration, or no-action position is disclosed for the Foundation, Permian Labs, GPU Finance Ltd. or GPU Servicer LLC; jurisdiction of formation for GPU Servicer LLC is not stated.
- The identity of the "Tokenizing Agent" under the original CALIBER model was never disclosed.
- Whether USDai/sUSDai minting currently imposes KYC on retail users (beyond sanctions screening and the Paxos-approved-minter gate) is not clearly documented.
